Features

Everything you need to catch risk early

A single system that turns raw academic data into clear, actionable signals for advisers, instructors, and administrators.

Continuous risk scoring

Attendance, GPA, failed subjects, and missed activities are combined into a single 0–100 risk score with transparent breakdowns.

Automated alerts

The moment a threshold is crossed, the system generates a severity-tagged alert so nobody slips through the cracks.

Intervention tracking

Log counseling, tutoring, parent conferences, and coaching — then track outcomes to see what actually helps each student.

Institution-wide analytics

Administrators get aggregate views by program, section, and risk level to guide resourcing and policy decisions.

Role-based dashboards

Advisers, instructors, students, and administrators each get a tailored view — no clutter, no irrelevant data.

Private by design

Students see their own status. Advisers see their advisees. Admins see aggregates. Access always matches responsibility.
